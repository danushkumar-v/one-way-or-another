What we have to predict

Four values, three files

1 · Direction of travel

blank links. Each gets F, T or B: one-way away from its southern end, one-way towards it, or both ways.

2 · Turn restrictions

from → to
An ordered list of links: the one you come from first, then the one you may not enter.

3 · Truck limit: type

which link
Which links carry a HEIGHT, WEIGHT or NO TRUCKS restriction.

4 · Truck limit: value

cm / kg
The number on the sign: height in centimetres, weight in kilograms.

Direction comes first. The other three depend on it: a turn only needs banning if it is legal, and a sign only governs the road it faces.

Road class, length and shape tell you that a street is probably one-way, never which way. So we tested on hidden areas, like the exam, and went looking for evidence from the street itself.

What we finalised

One method per target, each chosen on hidden areas

TargetFinal methodScore on the blank zoneTried and dropped
Direction of travel Two-stage LightGBM: is it one-way, then which way. Fed by Mapillary signs, drive-through photo sequences, a dual-carriageway rule, graph features 1 and 2 hops along the street, and collective classification (neighbours' predictions fed back, 2 rounds).
Then a flow-consistency solver (mixed-integer program): every junction needs a way in and a way out, twin carriageways run opposite, direction carries through 2-link nodes. All soft penalties.
one-way precision , recall , F-vs-T 		Road side of signs and markings (no gain) · hard rules instead of penalties · a bigger LightGBM · more feedback rounds
Turn restrictions LightGBM turn classifier: one row per possible turn at every junction, with geometry, Mapillary signs, lane arrows and traffic lights. Keep turns scoring above 0.7. Exit rule: a ban may never remove the last legal exit.
predictions right, F1 		Reading ban signs directly (almost never matches) · geometry without Mapillary
Truck limits Rules, no learned model: a Mapillary weight sign is assigned to the link it faces; the value is the most common one in the given rows.
new rows exactly right, 		HEIGHT and NO TRUCKS rows (too many false alarms) · fixed default values
